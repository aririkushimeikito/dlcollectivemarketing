Careers

Role: Social Media Intern

Time Commitment: Approximately 20 hours per week
Location: Hybrid, Central Florida (in-person shoots and meetings as needed)
Start Date: Ongoing (earliest availability)
End Date: TBD
Compensation: To be determined based on experience and term length

About DL Collective Photography LLC
DL Collective is a full-service marketing and content agency focused on creative direction, visual storytelling, and strategic branding. We work collaboratively with brands to produce thoughtful, elevated content across social media, photography, and video.

Responsibilities

  • Edit short-form video content for social platforms using CapCut or professional editing software such as DaVinci Resolve, Adobe Premiere Pro, or Final Cut Pro
  • Assist in managing internal social media accounts by writing captions, publishing content, and responding to comments and direct messages
  • Create simple, on-brand graphics using Canva
  • Appear on camera for internal content when needed, with occasional support on client-facing content
  • Assist during client photoshoots and events, including preparing shot lists and props and supporting setup and breakdown on shoot days
  • Maintain organization of internal and client assets and files
  • Monitor and respond to inbound emails in a professional and timely manner

Qualifications

  • Based in Central Florida
  • Experience editing social media content using CapCut or professional editing software such as DaVinci Resolve, Adobe Premiere Pro, or Final Cut Pro
  • Understanding of pacing, formatting, and optimization for social-first video content
  • Experience designing graphics in Canva with strong attention to brand consistency
  • Comfortable appearing on camera
  • Strong understanding of current social media trends, formats, and content strategy
  • Familiarity with Google Workspace (Drive, Docs, Sheets)
  • Open to feedback and able to apply direction efficiently
  • Strong organizational and multitasking skills
  • Ability to work independently while collaborating with a creative team
  • Flexible working schedule

How to Apply
Email DL.COLLECTIVE@OUTLOOK.COM with your full name and "Social Media Intern — 2026" in the subject line. Please include your resume.
