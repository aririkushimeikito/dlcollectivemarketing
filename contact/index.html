FAQs

Can we create a custom package instead of choosing a preset service?
Absolutely. Beyond our Essential Presence, Growth Strategy, and Premium Creative Partnership packages, we build custom combinations of services around your goals, timeline, and budget.
How do I get started?
Reach out through any of the options above and we'll set up a discovery call. We'll learn your business, goals, and audience, then build a strategy and scope tailored specifically to you.
How long does it take to start services?
After our discovery call and onboarding, most engagements begin within one to two weeks, depending on scope and scheduling.
What locations do you service?
We're based in Winter Park, Florida, serving businesses throughout Florida and national clients across the United States. We travel for shoots, events, and on-site production whenever a project calls for it.
What types of businesses do you work with?
We partner with service-based companies, retail and consumer brands, hospitality, real estate, professional services, events, and growing startups.
Do you offer standalone services or only monthly packages?
Both. Many clients start with a standalone project — like a photo or video shoot — and grow into an ongoing monthly partnership as their needs expand.
What makes DL Collective different from other marketing agencies?
We combine creative direction with data-driven strategy, delivering cohesive content and marketing that feels intentional, authentic, and built to grow your brand.
Do you carry General Liability Insurance and Workers' Compensation insurance?
Yes. We carry General Liability and Workers' Compensation insurance, and can provide a certificate of insurance when your venue or project requires one.
Do you have drone insurance and an FAA Part 107 license?
Yes. Our drone work is covered by aviation insurance and flown by an FAA Part 107 certified pilot, so your aerial content is captured safely and legally.
