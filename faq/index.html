Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with DL Collective. Still have a question? Reach out anytime.

What services does DL Collective offer?
We're a full-service marketing collective. Our work spans content creation, social media management, paid ads, branding, email marketing, SEO, influencer marketing, and photography & videography — delivered as standalone projects or an integrated strategy.
Where are you located, and do you travel?
We're based in Winter Park, Florida, and work with clients across the country. We travel for shoots, events, and on-site production whenever a project calls for it.
How do we get started?
Book a discovery call through our contact page. We'll learn your business, goals, and audience, then build a strategy and scope tailored specifically to you.
Do you offer one-off projects or ongoing partnerships?
Both. Many clients start with a standalone project — like a photo or video shoot — and grow into an ongoing monthly partnership as their needs expand.
How much does it cost?
Pricing depends on scope and deliverables. After our discovery call we'll send a custom proposal — no templated packages, just what your brand actually needs to grow.
How soon will we see results?
Production work is delivered on an agreed timeline. Growth-focused work — social, paid ads, and SEO — typically builds momentum within the first few months as we test, learn, and optimize.
