FAQs

Can we create a custom package instead of choosing a preset service?
Yes. Many clients choose to build custom service combinations based on their needs. You can select and combine individual services through our Contact page, or schedule a tailored consultation where we build a strategy and service structure around your goals, timeline, and budget.
How do I get started?
Getting started is simple. Reach out through our contact form or schedule a call—we'll walk you through the next steps and answer any questions along the way.
How long does it take to start services?
Onboarding is designed to be efficient and straightforward. In most cases, services can begin immediately after onboarding is completed, and in some cases faster project starts are possible depending on scheduling and project scope.
What locations do you service?
DL Collective is based in Winter Park, Florida, and currently serves businesses throughout Florida as well as national clients across the United States. We regularly travel for onsite content production, photography, videography, and marketing engagements when needed.
What types of businesses do you work with?
We work with a wide range of businesses including service-based companies, retail brands, hospitality, real estate, professional services, and growing startups. Most clients partner with us when they need consistent content, stronger branding, or ongoing marketing execution.
Do you offer standalone services or only monthly packages?
Both. Clients may hire DL Collective for standalone services such as photography, videography, drone content, paid ads, or marketing materials, or engage in ongoing partnerships through our monthly marketing packages.
What makes DL Collective different from other marketing agencies?
DL Collective combines creative production and marketing strategy under one team. This allows photography, video, branding, and marketing execution to remain visually and strategically aligned rather than outsourced across multiple vendors.
Do you carry General Liability Insurance and Workers' Compensation insurance?
Yes. DL Collective maintains General Liability Insurance coverage, and Workers' Compensation coverage where required for our company, employees, and contractors. If your business or property requires proof of insurance, a Certificate of Insurance (COI) can be provided upon request prior to or at the time of onsite services.
Do you have drone insurance and an FAA Part 107 license?
Yes. DL Collective carries drone liability insurance for commercial drone operations. Our drone pilots operate under an FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot Certificate for commercial flight operations. If requested, clients may receive a COI specific to drone operations.
