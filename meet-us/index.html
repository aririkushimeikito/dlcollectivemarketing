DL Collective is founded and led by Lauren Miller, combining creative direction with strategic
insight to help brands grow with clarity and purpose.

Lauren, holds a bachelor's degree in Advertising and a master's in Entrepreneurship from
the University of Florida. She brings a background in advertising and marketing, with
experience spanning agency work and national brand partnerships. Her foundation in
storytelling, creative direction, and campaign strategy drives DL Collective's approach to
building brands that feel intentional, authentic, and visually

cohesive.

Danly, co-founder holds a bachelor's degree in Business Administration from Southern New
Hampshire University. He brings a data-driven perspective shaped by his experience
supporting national sales teams at Fortune 500 companies through analytics and
performance strategy, specializing in translating data into actionable direction to support
measurable, long-term growth.

how we work

Our services are designed with flexibility in mind. Everything you need to
jumpstart your brand's marketing — to learn more or find the best fit for
your goals, contact us for a personalized consultation.

Discovery Call

We learn about your business, goals, and challenges to ensure alignment and
define the right direction moving forward.

Brand & Marketing Audit

We review your current brand, content, and marketing efforts to identify
opportunities for improvement and growth.

Strategy & Onboarding

We establish a clear plan, define priorities, and align creative and marketing strategy
before execution begins.

Collective Marketing Packages

Choose from one of our three subscription packages — Essential Presence, Growth Strategy or
Premium Creative Partnership — for ongoing support, or explore our add-on and stand-alone
services for one-time projects.
