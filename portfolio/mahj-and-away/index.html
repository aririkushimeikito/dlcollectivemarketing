Objectives / Goals

we partnered with Mahj & Away to bring the warmth of their game nights to the
feed. Mahjong can feel intimidating from the outside, so the goal was to make it
inviting: show newcomers they don’t need a partner, a set or any experience to
pull up a chair. We built a content system around the experience itself (beautiful
venues, hosted tables, new friendships formed over tiles) and paired it with clear,
elegant event promotion that turned interest into reserved seats.

content in action

From tile-side close-ups to elegant table settings, we turned Mahj & Away’s
game nights into a feed people wanted to be part of. Each piece of content
captured a real event, from the curated venues and hosted tables to the branded
gift bags and the friendships formed between rounds.

Short-form videos took viewers inside the night, from check-in to the first hand
to the final toast. Carousels broke down the basics of the game alongside
upcoming events, making it easy to go from curious to seated.
