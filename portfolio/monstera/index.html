The Brief

Monstera is a concept brand for a head spa and wellness sanctuary inspired by
the quiet power of tropical nature. We set out to create an identity that feels
both luxurious and grounded: calm enough to slow guests down, distinctive
enough to stand out in a crowded wellness market. The challenge was to capture
the feeling of stepping out of everyday life and into a space where time moves
gently.
